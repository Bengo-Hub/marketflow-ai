# Third-party content in the Marketing OS library

The playbooks, house rules and curated prompts embedded in this package are adapted from the sources below. The playbooks are short distillations written for MarketFlow, not copies; the curated prompts are selected entries.

## marketingskills

- Source: https://github.com/coreyhaines31/marketingskills (skills: ad-creative, ads, ab-testing, copywriting, copy-editing, social, video, image, emails, sms, content-strategy, marketing-plan, offers, launch, product-marketing, marketing-ideas, influencer-marketing, events, customer-research, competitor-profiling, lead-magnets, referrals, community-marketing, revops, sales-enablement, attribution, seo-audit, ai-seo, marketing-loops)
- Licence: MIT. Copyright (c) Corey Haines.

## Marketing Psychology Kit

- Source: https://github.com/grayskripko/marketing-skills (plugin marketing-psychology: dark-pattern-check, persuasion-audit)
- Licence: MIT. Copyright (c) 2026 Sergey Skripko.

## prompts.chat

- Source: https://github.com/f/prompts.chat (`prompts.csv`), curated by `cmd/curate-prompts` into `data/prompts.json`
- Licence: prompt content is dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0 Universal.
